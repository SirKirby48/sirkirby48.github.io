Game

Fundamentals 1
Students will understand the relevant history of video games.
Students will be able to understand basic game design, user
experience, and user interface concepts.
Students will be able to identify various game platforms, understand
their impacts, and develop at least one game on one of those
platforms.
Students will define various game genres and types
Students will be able to create and develop a game, in one of the
identified game genres (Action, Adventure, RPG (Role Playing Game),
Simulation or Strategy), using the Game Development Production
Pipeline.
Students will explore careers and training in the game design and
production world
