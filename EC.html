Echo Chambers is a dungeon-escapade side-scroller game, in which you play as Ruby, who does not know how she became trapped in the Echo Chambers.
As of May 2026, Echo Chambers has 5 levels, 1 sub-boss, 1 (of the three) servant bosses, and 1 unique enemy (there were going to be around eight unique enemies, 4–6 sub-bosses, 3 servant bosses, one final boss, 20 levels—divided into 4 tiers of 5 levels—and at least 5 cutscenes, but due to time constraints, much content was cut for the time being).
As the lead programmer, lead developer, and lead designer, I had done the vast majority of the programming, the asset and team managements, and a bunch of other tasks as well, such as advertising.
