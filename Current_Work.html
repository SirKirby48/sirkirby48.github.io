My Current Work


This is my final project for Year I, named "Under the Strip," made by ManaForge (my team's name). Under The Strip was a retro-like puzzle-game similar to Sokoban with extra puzzles (for those unaware of what Sokoban is, it is a push-box top-down game). Under The Strip was set in the 1960's, in a casino called "The Golden Strip." The game was about a gambler that made a fortune with cards, and the owner of The Golden Strip trapped them under the casino, forcing him to solve puzzles "with dice rolls" to make a way out.
